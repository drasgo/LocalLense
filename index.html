Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about LocalLens.

No, absolutely not. Everything runs with ONNX Runtime Web directly in your browser. Your images never leave your device – there are no servers involved in the processing. The only network requests are to load the AI models on first use.
Performance depends on your device's hardware. If you have a modern GPU, ONNX Runtime Web may use WebGL/WebGPU acceleration. On older devices or those without GPU support, processing falls back to CPU, which is significantly slower. Large images also take longer – for best results, try images under 2000px. First runs are slower because models must be downloaded.
